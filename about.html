THE HEART OF JOJJY

Faithfulness is rarely glamorous. Most of following Jesus is learning to walk steadily through hospital corridors, kitchen tables, and quiet Tuesday afternoons.

The Longer Story

Ordinary faith, written slowly.

I grew up in a small town where faith was simply part of the furniture: present, unremarkable, and quietly sustaining. I was ordained at twenty-six, certain I understood the Gospel. By thirty, I had discovered how little I knew about how it actually worked in a real life.

For two decades I sat with people in hospital waiting rooms, at kitchen tables, through divorces and funerals and quiet crises of faith. I preached on Sundays and visited on Tuesdays. I learned that the questions people actually carry are rarely the ones they ask in church.

When I left pastoral ministry, my wife suggested I write down what I had learned. Not a theology textbook. Not a self-help manual. Just honest words for people trying to follow Jesus between the interruptions.

That is what Jojjy is — a personal publication of short, honest reflections. Short enough to read in a quiet moment. Long enough, I hope, to stay.

“The questions people carry in their hearts are rarely the ones they have the courage to ask on Sunday morning.”

— George, from the countryside desk

Milestones

A steady progression.

Twenty-five years of listening, pastoring, questioning, and learning to write what remains true.

1998

Ordained as pastor

Began pastoral ministry in a small rural congregation. Twenty-three years of Sundays and ordinary Tuesdays followed.

2004

Season of doubt

A difficult year that shattered easy answers and reshaped how I read Scripture, pray, and understand grace.

2011

Started keeping a journal

What began as private morning notes in a battered notebook eventually became the bedrock for these essays.

2021

Left formal pastoral ministry

Traded the pulpit for a wooden desk by a field and began listening and writing in earnest.

2023

Launched Jojjy

The Sunday letter began with twelve readers and now reaches thousands of ordinary disciples seeking quiet truth.

What Shapes This Writing

Three convictions I write from.

Conviction 01

Rooted in Scripture

These essays are not self-help dressed in spiritual vocabulary. They begin with the Bible, not as an intellectual proof-text, but as a living word that walks beside us in grief, work, and praise.

Conviction 02

Grounded in everyday

Faith that cannot survive a sink of dirty dishes or a difficult conversation at work is a brittle thing. I write about following Jesus where we actually live: the commute, the kitchen, and the quiet Tuesday afternoon.

Conviction 03

Honest before God

I have no interest in presenting a tidier version of faith than I actually possess. God can be trusted with our exhaustion, our questions, and our half-finished prayers.
